Why I created The Alert Room

An alert doesn'tcome with ananswer key.

I built this because knowing a red flag and making a defensible decision are two very different skills.

Working in fraud operations, transaction monitoring, and customer due diligence taught me that the hardest part isn't spotting something unusual. It's knowing what to do next.

An alert is only a starting point. You have to understand the customer, follow the transactions, question what doesn't fit, and explain your conclusion—even when the answer isn't obvious.

I wanted that kind of practice to be accessible without needing access to a bank's internal systems or real customer information. So I created The Alert Room: a free, fictional investigation lab where you can open a case, test the evidence, write your decision, receive QA feedback, and try again.
